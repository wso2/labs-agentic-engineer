> **Rationale:** Provides the role-specific screens for Employees, Managers and Finance to submit, review and export claims via expense-api.

## Scope

Implement the expense-webapp React SPA per `specs/design/components/expense-webapp/design.json`, driven entirely by `expense-api` (no business logic in the webapp) and gated by sign-in through the `user-auth` Thunder platform-resource (gate: provisioning of `user-auth`).

Screens: EmployeeClaims, NewClaim, ClaimDetail, ManagerQueue, ClaimReview, FinanceApproved, ExportHistory

Flows:

- [ ] **F1 · My Claims**
  An employee submits a claim and tracks it through to a decision
  Persona: Employee
  Stories: 1, 2, 3
  Walk: EmployeeClaims → NewClaim → ClaimDetail
- [ ] **F2 · Approvals**
  A manager reviews and decides on claims from their team
  Persona: Manager
  Stories: 4, 5, 6
  Walk: ManagerQueue → ClaimReview
- [ ] **F3 · Payroll Export**
  Finance reviews approved claims and exports them to payroll
  Persona: FinanceReviewer
  Stories: 7, 8, 9
  Walk: FinanceApproved → ExportHistory

No flow: none — stories 1-9 are all walked above.

## Acceptance

- Every screen in `wireframes.dsl` is implemented element-for-element (buttons, tables, inputs) and every navigation arrow works.
- EmployeeClaims/NewClaim/ClaimDetail call `expense-api`'s `/me/claims*` endpoints; edit/withdraw controls are disabled or hidden once a claim is no longer pending.
- ManagerQueue/ClaimReview call `/me/team/claims*`, showing only the manager's team, with approve/reject (with reason) actions.
- FinanceApproved/ExportHistory call `/claims` and `/exports`, and the "Export to Payroll" action triggers `POST /exports` and downloads the resulting CSV.
- All API calls pass the signed-in user's token from the Thunder sign-in flow; unauthenticated users are redirected to sign-in.
