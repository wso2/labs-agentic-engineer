> **Rationale:** Owns expense claim lifecycle, team-scoped approval and payroll export, and needs to exist before the webapp can be wired to it.

## Scope

Implement the expense-api Ballerina service per `specs/design/components/expense-api/design.json` and `specs/design/components/expense-api/openapi.yaml`:

- `EMPLOYEE` / `EXPENSE_CLAIM` / `EXPORT_BATCH` persistence per `specs/design/domain-model.md`, backed by the `expense-db` postgres-cnpg platform-resource (gate: provisioning of `expense-db`).
- `/me/claims` (list, submit), `/me/claims/{claimId}` (get, patch), `/me/claims/{claimId}/withdraw` — Employee-scoped claim lifecycle (submit, edit while pending, withdraw while pending).
- `/me/team/claims` (list), `/me/team/claims/{claimId}/approve`, `/me/team/claims/{claimId}/reject` — Manager-scoped review restricted to the caller's direct reports (via `EMPLOYEE.managerId`).
- `/claims` (list all) and `/exports` (list, create) — Finance-scoped views and payroll CSV export; export marks included claims with `exportBatchId` so they are never exported twice.
- `/health` liveness endpoint.
- OAuth2 scope enforcement per operation as declared in the `securitySchemes` block (`claims:read`, `claims:submit`, `claims:update`, `claims:withdraw`, `claims:read-team`, `claims:approve`, `claims:reject`, `claims:read-all`, `exports:read`, `exports:create`), gated by sign-in through the `user-auth` Thunder platform-resource (gate: provisioning of `user-auth`).

No file/receipt storage, no email notifications — out of scope per the PRD.

Stories: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 — all served by this API; no UI screens live here.

## Acceptance

- All paths in `openapi.yaml` are implemented with matching request/response shapes and status codes (200/201/400/401/404).
- A claim's status transitions only follow pending → approved/rejected/withdrawn; edit/withdraw/approve/reject are rejected (400) when the claim is not pending.
- A Manager's team-scoped endpoints only return/act on claims from employees whose `managerId` is the caller.
- An export batch includes only approved, not-yet-exported claims and marks them exported; a claim never appears in two export batches.
- Every non-`/health` operation enforces the scope declared in the spec and rejects unauthenticated calls with 401.
