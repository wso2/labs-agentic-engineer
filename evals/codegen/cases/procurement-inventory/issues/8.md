> **Rationale:** Build-order edge to inventory-service; gated on platform resources user-auth (thunder-app) and procurement-db (postgres-cnpg).

## Scope
Implement the Ballerina procurement-api per specs/design/components/procurement-api/design.json and openapi.yaml: purchase requests (own and all, status filter, price captured from inventory-service, pending on creation, at least one line), approve/reject (limit 5000 from configuration, no self-approval, 409 on non-pending, 422 on rule breach), goods receipts (approved/partially-received only, up to outstanding quantity, partial receipts, calls inventory-service stock-movements with receipt id as reference), approval-limit, and catalogue/stock/ledger/low-stock endpoints proxied to inventory-service. Enforce the scopes in security.json (specs/design/security.json) per operation; sign-in via user-auth (platform-resource thunder-app, gate platform-authored). Data in procurement-db (platform-resource postgres-cnpg, gate platform-authored). Depends on inventory-service for build order.

No flow: backend API, no views (stories 1-12 are walked in procurement-webapp).

## Acceptance
- All operations implemented with documented codes and scope checks.
- Approval, receiving and visibility rules hold (own requests only under /me).
- bal build && bal test pass.
