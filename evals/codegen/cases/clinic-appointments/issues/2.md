> **Rationale:** Build-order edge to clinic-api; gated on user-auth (thunder-app) provisioning.

## Scope
Implement the React SPA per specs/design/components/clinic-webapp/design.json and wireframes.dsl, calling clinic-api (build-order dependency) and signing in via Thunder (user-auth platform-resource gate, provisioned by the platform). Screens gated by caller scopes.

Screens: MyAppointments, BrowseSlots, RescheduleAppointment, DoctorSchedule, MySlots, PublishSlots, VisitNote, FrontDesk, BookForPatient

Flows:

- [ ] **F1 · My appointments**
  A patient books, reschedules and cancels appointments
  Persona: Patient
  Stories: 3, 4, 5, 6, 7
  Walk: MyAppointments → BrowseSlots → RescheduleAppointment
- [ ] **F2 · My day**
  A doctor publishes slots, reviews the day and writes visit notes
  Persona: Doctor
  Stories: 1, 2, 10, 11, 12
  Walk: DoctorSchedule → VisitNote → MySlots → PublishSlots
- [ ] **F3 · Front desk**
  A receptionist books for patients and checks them in
  Persona: Receptionist
  Stories: 8, 9
  Walk: FrontDesk → BookForPatient

No flow: story 13 (sign-in — platform SSO via Thunder owns the page; the app only redirects and gates screens by scope). Note: receptionist reschedule/cancel buttons on FrontDesk have no target screen; implement them in place (story 8).
