> **Rationale:** Owns seat capacity, registrations and ordered waitlist with promotion (stories 10-15); gated on registration-db and user-auth platform resources.

## Scope
Implement the Ballerina registration-service per specs/design/components/registration-service/design.json and openapi.yaml: session capacity (setSessionCapacity), availability, seat reservation with FIFO waitlist ordered by joinedAt, event attendee registration (no capacity, idempotent on duplicates -> 409), cancellation with immediate promotion of the first waitlisted entry, per-user registration listing. Verify the gateway's signed assertion. Intranet only; called only by events-api. Persist in registration-db (platform-resource gate: provisioning flagged by platform, no Task). user-auth (thunder-app) is also a platform gate.

No flow: backend service, no views.

## Acceptance
- Every operation in openapi.yaml implemented and matching the contract.
- Waitlist promotion occurs on cancel; positions renumber.
- bal build && bal test pass.
