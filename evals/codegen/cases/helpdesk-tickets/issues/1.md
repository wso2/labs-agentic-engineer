> **Rationale:** Single backend serving all stories; its platform-resource gates (user-auth thunder-app, helpdesk-db postgres-cnpg) are provisioned by the platform, not Tasks.

## Scope
Implement the Ballerina service per specs/design/components/helpdesk-api/design.json and openapi.yaml: all operations (/me, /me/tickets*, /tickets*, /agents), scope-gated per security.json, verifying the gateway's signed assertion. Persist tickets, comments and user profiles (recorded on first use) in helpdesk-db. Compute slaDueAt from priority (recomputed on triage), derive slaBreached, enforce lifecycle transitions (open→triaged→in_progress→resolved→reopened; 409 otherwise), owner-only access under /me (404 for others), queue filters (status, priority, assigneeId, slaBreached) and pagination.
Gates (platform-provisioned, no Task): user-auth (thunder-app), helpdesk-db (postgres-cnpg).
Stories: 1–12.
No flow: backend service, no views.

## Acceptance
- bal build && bal test pass.
- Every operation in openapi.yaml implemented with the documented status codes and scopes.
- Lifecycle, SLA and ownership rules match domain-model.md.
