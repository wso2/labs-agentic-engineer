> **Rationale:** SPA for all three roles calls helpdesk-api (build-order edge); user-auth thunder-app is a platform provisioning gate.

## Scope
Implement the React SPA per specs/design/components/helpdesk-webapp/design.json and wireframes.dsl, using Oxygen UI, signing in via SSO (user-auth thunder-app gate, platform-provisioned) and calling helpdesk-api per its openapi.yaml; screens gated by the operation they load.

Screens: MyTickets, NewTicket, MyTicketDetail, Queue, TicketWork, AssignTicket, TeamQueue, LeadTicket, ReassignTicket

Flows:

- [ ] **F1 · Raise and follow a ticket**
  An employee raises a ticket, follows it and may reopen it
  Persona: Employee
  Stories: 1, 2, 3, 4, 12
  Walk: MyTickets → NewTicket → MyTicketDetail
- [ ] **F2 · Work the queue**
  An agent triages, assigns, works and resolves tickets
  Persona: Support Agent
  Stories: 5, 6, 7, 8, 9
  Walk: Queue → TicketWork → AssignTicket
- [ ] **F3 · Watch the queue**
  A team lead watches SLA breaches and reassigns tickets
  Persona: Team Lead
  Stories: 10, 11
  Walk: TeamQueue → LeadTicket → ReassignTicket

No flow: none — stories 1–12 are all walked above (sign-in/out handled by platform SSO).

## Acceptance
- Build and lint pass; every screen and flow above walkable in mock mode.
- Role-specific screens load only for permitted roles.
