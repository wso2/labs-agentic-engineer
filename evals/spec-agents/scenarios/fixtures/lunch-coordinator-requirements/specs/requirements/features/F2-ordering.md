# Ordering

## Purpose

Teammates add what they want to the open round, and change their minds, until
it closes.

Needs: F1.

## User Stories

- F2.1 As a teammate, I add one or more items to the open round, each with a description, a quantity and a price, before its cutoff.
- F2.2 As a teammate, I edit or remove the items I added while the round is still open.

## Decisions

- An item added after the cutoff is refused with a clear message.
- A teammate cannot edit or remove another teammate's items.
- Each teammate types the price of their own item, so totals are only as accurate as what people type. *assumed*
