# Slack notifications

## Purpose

The team hears about a round in its existing Slack channel.

Needs: F1.

## User Stories

- F4.1 As a teammate, I get a message in the team's Slack channel when a round opens.
- F4.2 As a teammate, I get a message in the team's Slack channel when a round reaches its cutoff.

## Decisions

- Slack is the only notification channel.
