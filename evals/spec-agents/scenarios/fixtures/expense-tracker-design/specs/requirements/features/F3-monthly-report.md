# Monthly report

## Purpose

Finance reads the month's total of approved claims from the same record.

Needs: F2.

## User Stories

- F3.1 As an Approver, I want to read the monthly total of approved claims, so that Finance can close the month without rebuilding the figure by hand.

## Decisions

- The report is the total of approved claims per calendar month, read-only, with no export in this release.
