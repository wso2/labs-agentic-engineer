# Approvals

## Purpose

An approver works one queue of submitted claims and decides each one.

Needs: F1.

## User Stories

- F2.1 As an Approver, I want to see every claim waiting for a decision in one queue, so that nothing sits unanswered.
- F2.2 As an Approver, I want to approve a submitted claim, so that the employee is reimbursed and the claim leaves the queue.
- F2.3 As an Approver, I want to reject a submitted claim with a reason, so that the employee knows why and can correct it.

## Decisions

- A decision is final: an approved or rejected claim cannot be decided again. *assumed*
