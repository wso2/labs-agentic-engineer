# Acme Travel & Expense Policy, version 3

## 1. Scope

This policy covers expenses staff pay themselves and claim back. Corporate
card transactions are reconciled separately by Finance each month and are not
claimed through this process.

## 2. Receipts

A receipt is required for any expense above $25. A photo of the receipt is
enough; the paper original does not need to be kept.

## 3. Limits

Meals are capped at $50 per person per day. Hotel stays are capped at $180 a
night in any city.

## 4. Approval

Every claim is approved by the employee's line manager. Claims over $1,000
also need a second approval from Finance after the manager.

## 5. Payment

Approved claims are paid with the next monthly payroll. Finance posts approved
claims to Xero every night.

## 6. Retention

Expense records, including receipts, are kept for seven years.

## 7. Mileage

Mileage is reimbursed at $0.30 a mile for business travel in a personal car.
Mileage is handled by a separate form this year and is not part of the new app.
